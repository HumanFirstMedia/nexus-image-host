The scenario.

Fit Buddha is preparing a “New to Lagree” campaign for Santa Barbara and Ventura. The team has a homepage, emails, front desk language, Instagram captions, and instructor talking points. The risk is familiar: too much spiritual fog, not enough concrete class value.

Starting point

People are curious but unsure.

They do not know the Megaformer. They worry class will be too hard, too elite, or too embarrassing.

Brand opportunity

Make the room feel safe and strong.

Lead with expert coaching, modifications, low-impact intensity, and the feeling of being recognized when they walk in.

Business outcome

Move them to the intro offer.

The immediate conversion goal is simple: try two classes for $35, then convert the second visit into a return rhythm.

SPORT in practice.

Use SPORT before publishing anything. If the piece fails one letter, fix that section before it goes live.

S - Studio truth

Does the piece name the real thing people buy? Lagree. Megaformer. 40 minutes. Strength, endurance, posture, core, low-impact intensity.

P - People in the room

Does it show belonging through behavior? Names, modifications, coach memory, beginner support, local regulars, friendly accountability.

O - Offer clarity

Is the next step visible and low-friction? “Try 2 classes for $35” should appear early and return after the main story beat.

R - Retention reason

Does it explain why people come back? Progress is noticed. The class is hard but safe. The room remembers them.

T - Tone check

Does it sound athletic, warm, local, and grounded - not mystical, generic, or overpromising transformation?

The sample campaign decision.

Brannon reviews two campaign directions. SPORT makes the winning option obvious because it ties creative choices directly to business outcomes.

Reject

“Awaken your inner transformation.”

Too vague. No machine. No class length. No proof. No local reason to trust the studio. It could belong to any wellness brand.

Approve

“Hard class. Warm room. Easy first yes.”

Concrete, human, and conversion-ready. It respects the body, names the room, and makes the intro offer feel approachable.

Come for the burn. Stay because they know you.

This is the strategic center of the Fit Buddha direction: athletic first, community second, transformation earned through consistency.
